## Scope

Covered by this licence:

- the recorded material in `Voice_audio/`,
- the derived databases `voices/usd16/usd16` and `voices/usd/usd`,
- the metadata and provenance files in those folders.

Not covered, and not relicensed by this file:

- the MBROLA engine, which is AGPL v3.0,
- the eSpeak NG descriptor (`mb-usd16`, `mb-usd`) and phone translation table
  (`usd_phtrans`), which are part of eSpeak NG and GPL v3.0,
- the add-on code, which is GPL v2.0,
- the MBROLA database file format itself.
